## What makes the top bots different

Caveat first: the showdown is the FINAL's format (free-for-all between top bots), not our practice format (one
of us against a 15-bot squad that hunts us). Items are ranked by the points I think they would gain us in
**practice**, where we are scored now; each is backed by SEEN timestamps.

1. **They are much tougher: cubes and plating.** sonion had 26 cubes and max armor 2981, i.e. the 10-cube cap
   plus plating three times (C 3:46). 2.3% took plating at its first offer (D 1:34: 1739 = 1 plating, 2 cubes).
   Our bot, in the simulator, ends with about 2 cubes and 1 upgrade, and its upgrade order puts armor (-8 %
   damage) before plating (+15 % max HP; plating is worth more effective HP: 1.15 vs 1/0.92 = 1.087).
   Our loot rule only collects cubes when no mech is within 120 m, before 260 s, and never while perched or
   climbing, so it mostly leaves the cubes a kill drops (a killed mech drops all its cubes plus 2).
   Expected gain: large. Every kill against the squad drops 2+ cubes; collecting them and stacking plating
   compounds over the match.
2. **They carry a laser early.** autocannon + laser on sonion (C 3:46), 2.3% (D 1:34) and kyoto-max (E 1:29);
   2.3% had it by 1:34. Our bot picks up a laser in 25-32 % of simulated matches (detour only within 55 m,
   before 260 s, and never from a roof). Expected gain: medium-large (315 hitscan damage at 280 m, no lead).
3. **They heal to full between fights.** 2.3% went from 722 to 1803 (full) between 2:21 and 3:06 without dealing
   damage (D). The winners of A and B kept their armor high to the end (sonion nearly full at A 3:49,
   giddygiddyboy full at B 3:30). Our bot re-engages any mech within 90 m and COVER is only for bad fights, so it
   often starts the next fight hurt. Expected gain: medium (fewer full-to-dead-in-15-s deaths, 31-34 % in the
   simulator).
4. **They avoid the centre brawl.** In B the final two sat together at the SE edge of the next circle from 0:11
   to the end while a central cluster fought (B 0:49, 1:28, 2:13). Our spot scoring pulls toward the centre and
   toward the highest roof. Expected gain in practice: small to medium (the squad hunts us wherever we are), but
   likely large in the final.
5. **Roofs are used, with a laser.** sonion fought from a roof edge with the laser (C 3:46-3:47 replay). Our
   PERCH is consistent with this; the difference is the laser and the HP pool, not the roof.
6. **Drones are a real threat even to top bots** ("A drone takes out Mahalas in 7th", C 2:38). Our bot keeps 55 m
   from drones it is not fighting, which looks right.
7. **The final is a bloodbath early**: 7 of 16 dead within 53 s (A), 10 of 16 by 1:48 (A), 6 left by 3:08 (B).
   Relevant for the final, not for practice.

Compared with our rules: FIGHT at 90 m (they fight a lot too, but with more HP and a laser); roof PERCH (fine,
seen used); COVER only in bad fights (they disengage to heal fully, which is different from cover-to-escape);
LOOT until 260 s and only when no mech is within 120 m (they keep collecting; sonion's 26 cubes mean it
collected kill drops all match).

## The 3 changes I would test first

1. **Collect kill drops and stack plating** (two separate experiments, plating-first is the smaller one):
   after a kill, pick up the dropped cubes within ~40 m even with other mechs around (unless outnumbered) and
   even from a roof, all match long; and pick plating before armor.
2. **Get the laser early**: in the opening, detour up to ~150 m for a laser when no mech is near it, and allow
   it from a roof.
3. **Heal to full before the next fight**: after a fight, if armor is below ~70 %, stay out of sight until regen
   (or a nearby supply drop) brings it back, instead of engaging the next mech within 90 m.
